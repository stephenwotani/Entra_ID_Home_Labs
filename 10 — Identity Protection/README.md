
# Microsoft Entra ID Identity Protection

**Domain:** Identity Threat Detection  
**Platform:** Microsoft Entra ID  
**Project Type:** Identity Security & Investigation Lab  


## 1. Project Overview

This project explores Microsoft Entra Identity Protection, focusing on identity risk signals, risky users, risky sign-ins, investigation workflows, and risk remediation.

## 2. Objectives

- Understand user risk and sign-in risk.
- Explore risk detections.
- Investigate suspicious sign-in activity.
- Review risk-based policies.
- Understand identity remediation workflows.

## 3. Tools Used

- Microsoft Entra Admin Center
- Microsoft Entra Identity Protection
- Risky Users and Risky Sign-ins
- Test tenant

## 4. Implementation Steps

1. Review Identity Protection availability and licensing.
2. Navigate to the risky users dashboard.
3. Explore risky sign-in information.
4. Review available risk detections.
5. Examine risk details and investigation context.
6. Review user risk and sign-in risk policies where available.
7. Document remediation options.
8. Record observations and findings.

## 6. Testing & Validation

- Identify available risk information.
- Review detection details.
- Distinguish risk detections from confirmed compromise.

## 7. Security Considerations

- Do not fabricate security incidents.
- Protect usernames, IP addresses, and sign-in details.
- Investigate before taking remediation actions.
- Follow appropriate incident response procedures.

## 8. Licensing Note

Identity Protection capabilities and risk-based policies require appropriate licensing.

## 9. Lessons Learned

Document investigation workflows, risk interpretation, and remediation insights.

## 10. Conclusion

This lab demonstrates how identity risk signals can support detection, investigation, and remediation of identity-based threats.
